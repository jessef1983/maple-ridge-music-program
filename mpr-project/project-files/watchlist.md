## What this file is

Candidate listings under evaluation, in transit, or closed out. **A `WATCH-###` never becomes an `MPR-###`** — MPR IDs are permanent asset keys assigned by `instrument-inventory` Workflow 1 when the box is opened. A watchlist row closes with a pointer to the MPR ID it became, or with what it went for and why it got away.

**Losing rows stay.** The closing price of an auction lost is comp data, and nobody else is collecting it for this program.

## Vetting checklist

Before buying from anyone not in the table above:

- Feedback **98%+ with real volume** — 100% across nine sales says almost nothing
- **Item-as-described** detailed rating 4.9+ — the one that matters here
- **Return policy**, ideally seller-paid. It converts an unverifiable claim into a reversible decision
- **Category history** — a seller who moves instruments weekly knows what a leadpipe is; a general liquidator doesn't
- **Recent negatives read individually** — a pattern of description disputes is disqualifying; one shipping complaint is noise
- Japanese sellers: a **prefectural second-hand dealer license** means a real business
