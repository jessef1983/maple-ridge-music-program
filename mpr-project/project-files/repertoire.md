## Owned

| REP | Title | Composer / Arranger | Publisher | Grade | Format | Purchased | Vendor | Price | Coupa ref | Notes |

**Column notes:**

- **REP** — sequential, never reused. A retired or discarded title keeps its row with a note rather than freeing the ID.
- **Grade** — the publisher's stated grade. It is a filter, not a verdict; the ensemble-fit judgment lives in the recommendation, not in this number.
- **Format** — printed set, digital license, or hybrid. Record what was actually licensed, including the authorized copy count where one applies.
- **Coupa ref** — expense report ID and line item ID, written by `coupa-expense-reconciliation` once the purchase appears in Coupa. Blank means unreconciled, not unpaid.
- **Notes** — last programmed, how it went, which section carried it, whether it is worth repeating.
