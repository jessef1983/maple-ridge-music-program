1. **MPR ID collision fixed.** Draft used real MPR-081/082/083, which are already assigned to other fleet instruments (Jupiter flute, Yamaha clarinet, Olds tuba). This entry uses MPR-TBD for all three per onboarding rules — do not assign real IDs in CE.

3. **Maker corrected for Drum 3.** Draft read "Planet" as the maker with model "Concert Grand." Planet is a Falls Percussion tuning-gauge retrofit brand (the Exactone gauge), not a drum manufacturer. Drum 3 is W.F.L. Concert Grand, confirmed by its pedal casting; the Planet gauge is bolted-on aftermarket hardware.
4. **Photo grouping corrected.** Draft split 11 photos as 4/2/5 across the wrong drums (e.g., filed the EZ-Wheel retrofit shot under "MPR-082/Ludwig" and left `201157059` unlisted). Confirmed grouping by direct inspection is 4/5/5 across the three physical drums as described above.
5. **Era estimate added.** Draft left all three at no date estimate. Added c. 1937–1939, sourced to WFL's 1937 founding and the "PAT. PEND." status matching Ludwig's Timpano Adjuster patent (US 2,150,981) filing-to-grant window — flagged as a reasoned estimate, not a confirmed date.
