## Channel notes

Nothing here has been assigned a channel yet, but the shape of the decision:

- **Student plastic clarinets and student flutes** — low value, moderate shipping weight. Local sale avoids fees and shipping entirely; eBay reaches more buyers but the fees and the box eat a large share of a two-figure sale.
- **Saxophones** — higher value, worth shipping, worth eBay's reach. Confirm pitch first.
- **The unidentified and mixed-parts pieces** — honest "as-is / for repair or parts" listings, priced accordingly.
- **A bulk lot** is worth considering for the weakest clarinets. One transaction, one box, one buyer who repairs and resells. Less money per horn, far less handling.

### Material note — genuinely unresolved

Normandy was sold under **both** a wood line and a plastic "Reso-Tone" line (a market listing titled "Normandy Reso-Tone Clarinet" confirms the plastic version exists under the same brand name).
