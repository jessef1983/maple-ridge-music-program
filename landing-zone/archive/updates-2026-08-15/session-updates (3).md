## Research Notes

**Signature 2000 Custom Series:** Entry-level custom/intermediate hybrid trombone. Rose brass bells are less common than yellow brass on student models; indicates a step-up or intermediate-targeted instrument. The "2000" designation in Signature's lineup typically sits between student and full intermediate.

**Rotary valve stop repair:** Plastic stop mechanisms are wear items on older F-attachment trombones. Replacement is straightforward (part cost ~$20–50) and commonly done by repair shops. This is a low-priority service item — the trombone plays fine without it, though the rattle is annoying.

**Serial "26":** If this is the complete serial, it suggests a relatively early production run or a short-series model. Signature instrument dating is not well-documented in public sources, so mark as ⚠️ pending and note that the complete production history is unknown.
