## Research Corrections & Lessons Learned

### Errors Made (Session Context)

1. **Invented "Somebody's Honors" as unique provenance** — actually "50 MEDALS OF HONOUR" (standard Besson branding). Removed fabricated premium from valuation.
2. **Called felt washers "original leather pads"** — felt is a routine wear part, says nothing about originality.
3. **Called restoration "professional"** — valve bracket shift and rework evidence suggests amateur/DIY work.
4. **Stacked unverified positives onto valuation** at each step (fictitious engraving + fabricated restoration quality + "complete original valve set" premium). Each correction was necessary to arrive at honest pricing.
5. **Initial comp data was weak** — one 2014 eBay sale ($456) and one 1919 Besson ($525) are reference anchors, not precise comps. Used them to frame realistic ranges.

### What Worked

- Physical inspection (photos) + actual play testing revealed condition better than static assessment
- Serial dating via official Conn/Besson charts is reliable
- Maker's marks and distributor info are verifiable and add historical interest (without premium)
- Both instruments are honest, playable pieces — no hidden structural issues
