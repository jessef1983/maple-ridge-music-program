## Key findings this session

### Newly playable and clean
- **LOT-004** (Bundy II alto sax, 905114) — playable, all parts present. Confirmed rougher cosmetically in hand than photos alone suggested (heavy scratching, consistent with prior grading).
- **LOT-010** (Artley, 787905) — playable, Fair, no issues noted. Serial now photo-confirmed.

### Playable with a minor, cheap-fix issue
- **LOT-006** (Bundy 577 Resonite, 1324589) — mouthpiece missing cork and ligature screw. Cheap standard parts; "complete outfit" claim needs qualifying until fixed.
- **LOT-011** (Jean Baptiste JCL480, F5495) — missing ligature. Also comes with a genuine bonus: an extra barrel of a different length (for tuning), a real value-add. Case is ancient/well-worn.
- **LOT-013** (Bundy Resonite, 645-107) — missing a key bumper, causes an audible "clack." Cheap fix. Case old but solid, latches functional.
- **LOT-015** (Bundy, 225852) — loose low F# key, rattles. Likely a loose pivot screw, quick regulation fix. Case is old but in genuinely great cosmetic condition — comparable case-only listings run ~$40, worth calling out in the listing.

### Playable but with a real quality problem (likely pad leak)
- **LOT-009** (Vito Reso-Tone, CO2823-7212) — playable but doesn't sound great, possible leak. Original mouthpiece is missing (confirmed in hand); tested with a substitute.
- **LOT-014** (Artley 175, 249004) — playable, but top register is squeaky, likely a pad leak. Also resolved a brand mix-up this session: the bell is engraved "Artley" (confirmed in hand), while the mouthpiece separately carries an "American Music" stamp — two different, both-correct markings, not a hand-log error.

### Playable but rougher — compounding issues
- **LOT-012** (Bundy, 775674) — case is shot (padding falling out, latches worn but functional), a visible gap between bell and lower joint when assembled (possible tenon/bore mismatch, needs a closer look), sticky/noisy keys, and sound overall isn't great. Weakest of the "playable" lots.

### Not currently playable
- **LOT-005** (M. Masson, N.1673, resin body) — not playable: bridge key alignment needs adjustment. All parts confirmed present — this is a regulation/adjustment problem, not a missing-parts problem. Likely a quick, inexpensive fix; worth a repair quote from Appleseed or D&M before deciding repair-vs-as-is.
- **LOT-008** ("Bundy" 577, mixed-parts body) — missing several keys, including the bridge key. Not playable as assembled. Condition downgraded to Poor. New marketing angle logged: pair this non-playable body with a genuinely nice spare case (e.g., LOT-015's) and sell as a "project clarinet + quality case" combo rather than a bare broken body.

### Pricing flagged stale (not yet re-run)
- **LOT-007** (Normandy, 3621A) — confirmed missing **both** mouthpiece and barrel (previously only mouthpiece was known missing). The existing $45/$28 pricing assumed a single missing part and is now stale — realistic range likely closer to a for-parts comp than the complete-horn comp it was priced against.
