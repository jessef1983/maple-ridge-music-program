**Play-test result:** Playable with spare mouthpiece.

**Pricing impact:** Playable status confirmed, but regulation work needed (floppy keys, hard low C response). Pricing of $225 ask / $150 floor adjusted to reflect these known maintenance needs — no longer priced against clean, ready-to-play comps.

**Opened:** Clarification that neck serials are non-standard on student/intermediate saxes. Body serial (400654) is the identifying mark; absence of neck serial is normal and requires no follow-up.
