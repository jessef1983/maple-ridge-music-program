## Finish Safety Findings

### Lacquer

**Risk:** HIGH. Ultrasonic cavitation can peel lacquer, especially older or already-compromised finishes.

**Mitigation:**
- Pre-screen each instrument for existing lacquer damage before cleaning
- If lacquer is flaking/lifting, hand-clean only (Dawn dish soap + soft brush)
- Use music-specific ultrasonic cleaner (not industrial parts washers)
- Strict controls: keep temperature under 100°F, power at 50–70%, max 3 minutes per cycle
- Rinse and dry immediately

**Affected fleet:** MPR-022 (Nikkan YTR-334S, "nearly perfect" finish) — do NOT ultrasonic. Hand clean only.

### Silver Plate

**Risk:** MEDIUM. Silver plating can strip if adhesion is poor (especially older instruments or low-quality plating jobs).

**Mitigation:** Same as above (music-specific machine, conservative settings, temperature control).

**Note:** Modern Yamaha student/intermediate brass (YTR-###, YEP-###) have decent plating and should be safe with proper settings.

## Tank Sizing

### For Euphonium Cleaning

Main body section when laid out: ~20–24 inches
**Minimum internal tank length needed:** 32 inches (confirmed as adequate)

**Recommendation:** 30L tanks (VEVOR, CREWORKS) with ~32" internal length

**Why not smaller:** 10L tabletop units (~12" internal length) are too tight; would force awkward bending or require unnecessary disassembly.

**Why not larger:** 40+ gallon industrial units unnecessary for this fleet size.
